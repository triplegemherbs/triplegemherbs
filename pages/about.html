Background & Training

My herbal trajectory began with training at the East West School of Planetary Herbology. From there, I set off working as a community herbalist in herbal apothecaries from Florida to California. This work allowed me to connect hundreds of people to simple, accessible, and effective forms of plant medicine, and to see the effect of herbal medicines at the community scale.

I also hold a Bachelor of Science degree in Botany from California Polytechnic University Humboldt, and a Master of Arts degree in Ecology and Evolutionary Biology from Washington University in St. Louis. I am a scientist by training, but have always been an herbalist at heart, and I believe in using evidence-based knowledge alongside time-tested herbal traditions to provide holistic herbal care - always meeting the client wherever they are at.
